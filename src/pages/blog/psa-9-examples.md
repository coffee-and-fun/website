PSA 9 is the grade most cards get when their owner was expecting a 10.

That is not a criticism of anyone's cards. It is what the distribution looks like. A 9 is a card with exactly one findable thing, and almost every card has exactly one findable thing.

I own a PSA 9, bought alongside a 10 and an 8 to study for [the grading estimator project](/blog/how-psa-grades-pokemon-cards-research-guide/). Its one findable thing is centering: it measured around 58/42 on the front, which looks fine at a glance and does not survive a ruler.

## What a 9 actually is

One thing, and only one thing.

- Centering slightly outside the tolerance commonly quoted for a 10
- Or one corner very slightly soft under magnification
- Or one faint surface line
- Or a trace of whitening on a single corner tip

Not several of those. One. Two findable flaws generally puts you at an 8.

## The centering case, with a real number

My PSA 9's front centering measures roughly 58/42. Held in the hand it reads as centred. Measured, it is not.

The commonly quoted tolerances put a 10 at 55/45 and a 9 at 60/40 on the front. This card sits between them, which is exactly consistent with the grade it received. That is one data point supporting numbers PSA does not publish, and I want to be clear that is all it is: [one card, and an unverifiable table](/blog/psa-centering-tolerances/).

The practical lesson stands regardless of what the true tolerance is. **Measure the front before you submit.** It costs nothing, it is the only criterion you can assess objectively at home, and it is the most common reason a 10 becomes a 9.

## PSA 9 versus PSA 10, in money

This is why the one-flaw distinction matters so much.

A PSA 10 routinely sells for a multiple of a PSA 9 of the same card, and on chase cards the gap gets absurd. The physical difference between the two is often a fifth of a millimetre of border, or a corner tip you need a loupe to fault.

Two nearly identical objects, wildly different prices, and the difference is a judgement call made by a person you will never meet. Whatever you think of that, it is the market you are submitting into, so price your expectations at a 9.
