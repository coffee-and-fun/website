## Eight themes

Open **Settings → Appearance → Theme** and pick your mood:

- **Night** (the default), **Dracula**, **Coffee**, **Sunset**, **Dim**, **Nord**, **Synthwave**: and **Cupcake** if you like it bright.
- The whole editor re-skins instantly, preview included.

## Fonts

- **Settings → Editor font**: system, serif, JetBrains Mono, and an **OpenDyslexic** option for easier reading.

## The chaos zone

At the bottom of Settings lives a drawer of completely optional toys. **Everything is off by default.**

- 🐱 **Pet cat**: a pixel cat wanders your editor. She reacts if you click her.
- 🟩 **Matrix rain**: write inside the code.
- 🌴 **Vaporwave mode**: A E S T H E T I C.
- 🔥 **Combo counter**: fighting-game combos for typing streaks.
- ⚡️ **Power mode**: keystrokes explode.
- ⌨️ **Typewriter sounds**: clack clack.
- 🎉 **Emoji rain**: it rains what you type.
- 🎵 **Music mode**: every key plays a note.
- 🚨 **Rage-quit alarm**: mass-deleting shakes the screen before you do something you regret.
- 📈 **WPM tracker**: type fast enough and it catches fire.

> Toys never touch your words. They're pure decoration on top of the editor. Switch any of them off and everything is instantly serious again.
