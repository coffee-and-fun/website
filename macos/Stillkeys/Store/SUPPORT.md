# Stillkeys support

Stillkeys gives you a timed cleaning screen for your Mac. Select a duration and click Start cleaning screen. Click Finish cleaning, hold Escape continuously for two seconds, or let the timer expire to return.

## Some keys still do things

The App Store edition captures typing in its foreground screen. It does not disable hardware or all macOS shortcuts. Power, Touch ID, media keys, protected system actions, and some global shortcuts remain available. Your mouse and trackpad are intentionally left available.

## The session ended early

Stillkeys releases input when your Mac changes focus, goes to sleep, switches user/session, or changes display configuration. In the Direct edition, revoked Accessibility access, a disabled event tap, or Secure Input can also end a session. Return to the home window and start another session when ready.

## Direct edition: keyboard blocking won't start

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and allow the stable installed copy of Stillkeys Direct. Return and click Check access again. If another app has a password field or secure keyboard mode active, close that field/mode before starting. Development builds that move or are signed again may need their permission entry refreshed. The App Store edition needs no Accessibility access.

## How should I clean my keyboard?

Follow your device manufacturer's instructions. Apple recommends shutdown and power disconnection before cleaning. Stillkeys does not replace those steps or make liquid cleaning safe. See https://support.apple.com/103258.
