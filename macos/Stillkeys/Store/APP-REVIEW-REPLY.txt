2. Purpose and target audience
Still Keys is a macOS utility for people who want to reduce accidental typing while tidying their keyboard. It provides a timed foreground cleaning screen that discards keyboard events delivered to the app. Users can choose a duration, keep the mouse or trackpad available, and finish manually or let the timer expire.

It does not disable keyboard hardware or every system shortcut. Power, Touch ID, media keys, and protected system controls remain available. The in-app guide explains these limits and links to Apple's cleaning instructions, including its recommendation to shut down and disconnect power before cleaning.

3. Setup and access
No account, credentials, sample files, internet connection, administrator access, or Accessibility permission is required by this Mac App Store build.

Launch the app, choose 30 seconds, and click "Start cleaning screen." Input capture begins immediately, with a three-second preparation countdown. Type ordinary letters or Return to exercise the foreground input capture. Click "Finish cleaning" with the mouse or trackpad to stop. Alternatively, hold Escape continuously for two seconds, or allow the timer to expire. A brief Escape tap does not end the session.

Click "Back to Stillkeys" to start again. "Cleaning guide" and "Private by design" are accessible from the home window. The available durations are 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, and 5 minutes.

The sandboxed build uses public AppKit local event monitoring and presentation options. It disables Command-Tab during a session. Command-Option-Escape remains available. Losing app focus, sleep, or a physical display configuration change ends the session.

4. External services, tools, and platforms
Core functionality runs locally using Apple's SwiftUI, AppKit, and Foundation frameworks. There is no backend, third-party SDK, authentication provider, data provider, AI service, advertising service, or analytics service. No typed text or key history is saved or transmitted.

The app is a one-time paid Mac App Store download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Optional links open Apple's cleaning guidance in the user's browser or Coffee & Fun support in the user's mail app; neither is needed for core functionality.

5. Regional differences
