## Description

A little pause. A cleaner keyboard.

Stillkeys gives stray keystrokes somewhere quiet to land while you take a short keyboard-cleaning break. Choose a time, open the cleaning screen, and return when you're ready.

A MOMENT THAT'S YOURS
Choose 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, or 5 minutes. The screen starts catching typing immediately, then gives you three seconds to get ready.

ALWAYS A WAY BACK
Click Finish cleaning with your mouse or trackpad, hold Escape for two seconds, or let the timer end. A change of focus, display, or sleep state ends the session automatically.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No advertising. No analytics. No keystroke recording. Stillkeys works on your Mac without an internet connection.

MADE FOR MAC
A quiet interface, light and dark appearances, clear controls, and a built-in guide. A small tool for a small moment of care.

PLEASE KNOW THE LIMITS
Stillkeys captures typing while its cleaning screen is active and disables Command-Tab for the session. It does not turn off your keyboard. Power, Touch ID, media keys, and some system shortcuts remain active. Your mouse and trackpad remain available.

Follow your device manufacturer's cleaning instructions. Apple recommends shutting down and disconnecting power before cleaning. Stillkeys does not replace those steps or make liquid cleaning safe.
