# Stillkeys App Store featuring nomination

Prepared September 13, 2026. Draft only; nothing has been submitted to Apple. The developer has confirmed App Review approval. Public release status and launch date still need confirmation.

## Nomination name (47/60 characters)

Stillkeys — A little pause. A cleaner keyboard.

## Nomination type

App Launch

## Description (942/1,000 characters)

Stillkeys turns an everyday Mac annoyance—accidental typing during keyboard cleaning—into a small, considered experience. Choose a timer, open the cleaning screen, and give your keys a little care while ordinary keystrokes are captured within the active screen. Finish with a click, hold Escape for two seconds, or let the timer end.

Built with SwiftUI and AppKit, Stillkeys pairs a soft lilac palette, rounded typography, and a playful keyboard illustration with clear controls and light and dark appearances. It opens straight to its purpose, without an account or permission setup.

Made by Coffee & Fun LLC, Stillkeys works offline with no ads, analytics, keystroke recording, or subscription. We’re nominating its Mac launch for people who appreciate focused utilities and thoughtful everyday design. Hardware controls and some system shortcuts remain active; the built-in guide explains the limits and links to Apple’s cleaning advice.

## Helpful details (447/500 characters)

We designed Stillkeys around one small task, giving equal attention to the first click and the way back. Four timer choices, immediate typing capture, a visible Finish button, and automatic session endings keep the flow simple. Native controls include accessibility labels for durations, the countdown, and Escape progress. Button feedback respects Reduce Motion. The app needs no Accessibility permission and never stores or transmits typed text.
